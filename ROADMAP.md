# Roadmap

The calendar already keeps showtimes out of **Em cartaz no período**, and Dia, Mês, and Agenda share one width. What is left is the noise around that frame.

## Quiet the house list

Each house is named four times: the introduction, the row of outbound links in the header, the filter chips, and the color legend in the footer.

Keep the chips. They are the control. Remove the header link row and the footer legend. A house page stays available from the session or exhibition sheet.

The introduction can name Cartaz without listing every house.

## Lighten the month cells

**Mês** still prints the title, the house, the subtitle, and the price inside each day. That column is now the same width as the day calendar, so a busy day becomes a tall stack.

Show the time and the title in the cell. Leave the house, the price, and the tickets on the selected day at the right.

## Stop Agenda from repeating the day

Agenda lists the month’s sessions on the left, and the right column lists the selected day again.

Keep the right column for Dia and Mês. Let Agenda use the full width of the same frame, with one list of sessions.
